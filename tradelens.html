Strategy definitions

EMA trend

EMA 9 above EMA 21 = short-term bullish alignment; below = bearish.

RSI momentum

RSI above 55 can support bullish momentum; below 45 can support bearish momentum. RSI above 70/below 30 can also signal stretched conditions.

VWAP positioning

Price above VWAP can indicate intraday strength; below can indicate weakness. VWAP uses available intraday candles.

Volume breakout

Latest candle volume at least 1.5× average of previous candles suggests unusual activity (not proof of a breakout).

MACD

MACD line vs signal line is used as a momentum confirmation; this scanner calculates it from candles.

Combined score

A rule-based score blends EMA alignment, RSI zone, VWAP, MACD and volume. It's a heuristic, not a prediction.
